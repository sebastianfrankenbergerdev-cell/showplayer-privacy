Privacy Policy for Show Player

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Show Player is a video player for Android that organises the videos on your device into shows, movies and playlists. It works entirely on your device, with your own files. It has no internet access, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. This policy explains what the app does with your videos and what it keeps on your device.

This policy applies to the Show Player app for Android (package com.showplayer.app), published by Sebastian Frankenberger, a single independent developer.

The short version

  • Your videos never leave your device through Show Player. I cannot see them.
  • The app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. I collect nothing about you.
  • Purchases, if you make any, are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details.

Videos and files on your device

Show Player reads the videos in your device's storage so it can list and play them. It uses Android's standard media access, which asks for your permission the first time. Depending on your Android version, you may be asked to allow access to all videos or to selected ones.

Everything Show Player does with your videos happens on your device:

  • Playing, browsing and sorting reads your files and does not copy them anywhere. To show lengths, thumbnails and the right orientation, the app reads technical details stored in the video files themselves (such as duration and resolution).
  • Grouping into shows and episodes works from folder and file names on your device.
  • Moving, renaming and deleting change files on your device only, and go through Android's own confirmation dialogs. Deleted videos go to a bin first, so they can be restored until the bin is emptied.
  • Folder access. For folders Android does not let the app write into directly, you can grant access once through Android's own folder picker. You can revoke it at any time.
  • Media management (optional). On Android 12 and later you can allow Show Player as a "media management app" in your phone's settings, so Android's confirmations for moving, renaming and deleting are answered without a dialog. This is off unless you turn it on, and it does not give the app any additional access to your data.
  • Tags for smart playlists are read from, and if you choose, written into, the video file's own metadata on your device. Those tags travel with the file if you later copy it elsewhere yourself.

Your settings, watch progress, shows, playlists, hidden folders and thumbnails are stored in Show Player's own private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

I have no access to any of this. There is no Show Player account, no cloud sync and no server I operate. Nothing described in this section is transmitted to me or to anyone else.

Purchases

Show Player may offer optional in-app purchases, such as an upgrade that unlocks extra features. Each purchase is a transaction handled entirely by Google Play. I never see or receive your payment details — not your card number, not your name, not your billing address.

To know whether a purchased feature is unlocked, the app asks Google Play whether the Google account on this device owns it, and stores the answer — a single yes or no — in the app's private storage on the device. That answer contains nothing about who you are.

Purchases are tied to your Google account, so reinstalling Show Player or moving to a new device can restore them. Google's Privacy Policy covers its processing of the payment itself.

Sharing with other apps

When you use Share or Open with, Show Player hands the selected video to the app you choose, using Android's standard sharing. This only happens when you ask for it. What the receiving app does with the video is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup for your Google account, Android may include Show Player's settings in that backup, so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is performed and stored by Google under your account and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. Your videos are not part of it, and I have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Your rights

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to export, correct or erase on request. If you are in the EEA or UK, the General Data Protection Regulation gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Show Player itself holds nothing on my side to which those rights could attach. For an Android backup, Google is the controller and you can exercise your rights directly with Google.

To remove everything Show Player stores on your device, uninstall the app or clear its data in Settings → Apps → Show Player → Storage.

Children

Show Player is not directed at children and I do not knowingly collect any data from children. The app contains no content of its own; it plays only the videos already on your device.

Security

Show Player's own data stays within the app's private storage, protected by Android's own sandboxing. Because the app has no internet access and nothing is transmitted to me, there is no server of mine that could be breached.

Changes to this policy

If Show Player gains a feature that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
